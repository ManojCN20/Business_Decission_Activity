MESSAGE 01

“My box arrived two days after the promised date.”

MESSAGE 02

“One pouch split open inside the box.”

MESSAGE 03

“Tracking said dispatched, but the box came late.”

MESSAGE 04

“I love the coffee. My refill arrived after I ran out.”

MESSAGE 05

MESSAGE 06

This small selected sample does not establish the experience of all customers. Consider severity as well as frequency.
